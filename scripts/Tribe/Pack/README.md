# Tribe/Pack

The pack of the land animal.

| File | Content |
| --- | --- |
| `PackKeeper.cs` | Component of `Main`: spawns wild kin on the island, steers them, lets them eat fruit, knows the offered action (call, nest, found the tribe), performs it on Space or the HUD button, F lets the pack stay or follow, a dead leader lives on in a member. |
| `PackEncounter.cs` | Keys 1 to 3 while leading a pack: court, display or hunt the closest animal of a foreign pack; adoption, tribute and meat. |
| `Kin.cs` | A conspecific: the player's body plan in a shade of its color, walks on land only, wild / following / staying / villager, pups grow up. |
| `Nest.cs` | The nest on the ground: mound, sticks, grass; the fire of the tribe once lit. |
